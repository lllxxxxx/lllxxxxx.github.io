StanceAgent-R1: Agentic Reinforcement Learning for Zero-Shot Conversational Stance Detection

Xing Li, Jinzhong Ning, Yijia Zhang

Findings of EMNLP 2026

Paper/PDF//

Conversational stance detection asks whether an utterance favors or opposes a target given its dialogue context. StanceAgent-R1 is an agentic reinforcement learning framework that decides, for each instance, whether to select evidence paths from the dialogue, elicit background knowledge, or both. It is warm-started on expert reasoning trajectories collected with rejection sampling, then trained with a reward for correct, well-structured, and route-consistent reasoning, and reaches state-of-the-art results on ZS-CSD, MT-CSD, and C-MTCSD.

Specialized Decision Models vs. General-Purpose LLMs: Benchmarking Jev Across Knowledge, Reasoning, and Multilingual Tasks

Xing Li, Qingcheng Chang, Jinzhong Ning, Changfeng Xu, Shenlong Zhang, Yijia Zhang, Ling Luo, Hongfei Lin

arXiv preprint, 2026

arXiv/PDF//

Jev is a "System One" decision model that picks an answer from given options instead of generating text. We evaluate it on 13 multiple-choice benchmarks covering knowledge, reasoning, and multilingual understanding, against 19 LLMs in frontier, representative, and small tiers. Jev matches frontier models on knowledge and commonsense tasks and scores best on MMLU-Redux and ARC-Challenge, but trails all 19 LLMs on MathQA, suggesting such models suit knowledge-based decisions more than multi-step calculation.

Can Decision Models Understand Stance? Evaluating Jev Against General-Purpose LLMs

Xing Li, Jinzhong Ning, Yijia Zhang, Liang Yang, Hongfei Lin

arXiv preprint, 2026

arXiv/PDF//

We evaluate Jev, a decision model that returns probability distributions over predefined options, on two stance detection datasets: VAST (English texts) and ZS-CSD (Chinese conversations). Compared with four general-purpose LLMs and two fine-tuned models, Jev performs on par with GPT-5.6 on VAST but falls behind stronger LLMs on ZS-CSD, especially in distinguishing favor from against.
