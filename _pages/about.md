I am a master's student at [Dalian Maritime University](https://www.dlmu.edu.cn/), working on natural language processing and multimodal learning.

My current research interests include:

- **Multimodal stance understanding** — how models infer a speaker's attitude toward a target from text, conversations, speech, and memes.
- **Memory for LLM agents** — how agents store, retrieve, and use long-term memory over extended interactions.
- **Vision-language navigation and embodied AI** — grounding natural-language instructions in visual perception and action.

Feel free to reach out by email if you would like to discuss research or potential collaborations.
